Lodging Option 1 - Stay at Las Brisas Resort and Villas

Calle Vista Lapas & Calle Hermosa, 800m Este de Costanera Puntarenas Playa Hermosa, de, Provincia de Puntarenas, Jacó, 61101, Costa Rica

Welcome to Las Brisas Resort and Villas, a peaceful boutique retreat in Jacó, tucked into tropical gardens just steps from Playa Hermosa's beautiful shores. Enjoy daily visits from monkeys, toucans, and macaws, two pools, and on-site spa services. Stay in a cozy room or a spacious villa with a full kitchen and private rooftop terrace, the perfect home base for your Costa Rica getaway.

Lodging Option 2 - Pacific Point Apartments - Beachside

Calle Bohio Playa Jaco Beachfront Puntarenas Jaco Beach, Garabito, Puntarenas Province, Jaco, 61101, Costa Rica

Welcome to Pacific Point Apartments, a sleek beachfront residence right in the heart of downtown Jacó, just steps from the sand on Bohío Street.

  • Building amenities: oceanfront pool, gym, co-working space, and rooftop BBQ terrace, plus nearby beachfront spas for massage and relaxation

  • Casino: walking distance to Croc's Casino Resort and Cocal Casino

  • Beach: steps from Playa Jacó for sun and surf

  • Nightlife & shopping: surrounded by top spots like Republik Lounge, Orange Pub, and The Green Room, plus restaurants, cafés, boutiques, and shops lining the streets

Lodging Option 3 - Private Villa in Punta Leona

Welcome to your villa in the Punta Leona area, tucked within a private 740-acre nature reserve on Costa Rica's central Pacific coast, surrounded by lush rainforest and resident wildlife like scarlet macaws and monkeys. Just steps away is Playa Blanca, a white-sand beach tucked between two cliffs, while downtown Jacó is about 15 minutes by car for casinos, nightlife, and shopping whenever you want a change of pace.

Day 4

Manuel Antonio National Park

Guided Tour in Manuel Antonio National Park

Guided tour in Manuel Antonio National Park

Explore Costa Rica's most beloved national park with a private Sneha Coastlines guide who handles your reservation and shows you all the best spots.

  • We handle the reservation: skip the hassle; entry tickets and timing sorted for you

  • Stunning white-sand beaches: the park has two of the best, Playa Manuel Antonio and Playa Espadilla Sur

  • Abundant wildlife: spot sloths, monkeys, iguanas, and colorful birds along the trails

  • Photo session: capture the beaches, wildlife, and views with your guide's help

Optional Activities at Manuel Antonio

  • Playa Biesanz: a secluded, calm crescent beach just outside the park, great for swimming, snorkeling, jetskiing, and parasailing

  • Nauyaca Waterfalls: a spectacular two-tiered waterfall with a swimming basin (about an hour south, if you want a bigger excursion)

  • Mangrove boat tour: wildlife-rich paddle or boat trip through the Damas Island estuary

  • Catamaran sunset cruise: snorkeling, dolphins, and sunset off the Manuel Antonio coast

  • Explore Quepos town: the lively gateway town nearby for shops, restaurants, and local color

  • Guided jungle hike: scenic rainforest trails to hidden viewpoints over the Pacific
