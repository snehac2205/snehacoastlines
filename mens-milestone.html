Why This Trip

Built for your crew, in the perfect place.

We took the same route as our Big Moments Tour and built it for groups of men: more adventure, more ocean, great grilled food and a party night just for you. You show up; we handle the rest.

Here's how we mark the moment, start to finish: a welcome toast on Day 1, the first pool party on Day 2, the full Celebration Night on Day 3, and a beach bonfire with a keepsake to take home on Day 4. The whole trip is the celebration, not just one night.

Adventure and downtime, on the same trip

Every day has some adrenaline — ATVs, zip-lining, surfing or sport fishing — plus time for the pool, a cold beer and good conversation. Everyone picks their own pace.

A real party

Night three is Celebration Night: a private cookout, a DJ, drinks and a toast to the guest of honor. It's the centerpiece of the trip, not just another dinner.

Three ways to stay

From a resort with a private room for each guest to a full villa in Punta Leona. One group per departure, 6 to 8 guests, priced by how you want to stay.

Nothing left to plan

Sneha Coastlines handles bookings, transportation and logistics from start to finish. You ride in our own vehicles — one or two, depending on group size — with drivers we trust.

The Journey

Five days, three places, one big celebration.

Sneha Coastlines' proven route along Costa Rica's Pacific coast — the same trusted drivers, stays and local partners. Activities are chosen with your group before the trip.

01
San José · Heredia Province

Arrival and Welcome Toast

VIP pickup at SJO airport and a private transfer to a great hotel in San José with a pool and gym. Before dinner, Melvin gathers the group for a welcome toast: everyone shares what they came to celebrate this week. A few days before the trip, Melvin has already talked with the guest of honor (or whoever is organizing), so the toast and Celebration Night are built around his real story. The night wraps up with a steak dinner at one of San José's best restaurants.

Private airport transferWelcome toast with MelvinOptional: San José nightlife
02
Jacó Beach · Puntarenas Province

Off to the Pacific and a Sunset Pool Party

A scenic drive to the Pacific coast, with a stop for a traditional Costa Rican breakfast on the way. Check in to the stay your group picked and, in the afternoon, a sunset pool party: music, cocktails, cold beer and the first toast of the trip.

Scenic coastal driveSunset pool partyOptional: casino night
03
Jacó

Pick Your Adventure — Then, Celebration Night

The morning starts with optional sunrise surfing or paddleboarding. Then everyone chooses: deep-sea sport fishing, an ATV ride through the mountains, zip-lining, parasailing or horseback riding for the adrenaline seekers; a hike through Carara National Park or a pool day for those who'd rather take it easy. When the sun goes down, the whole group comes together for the main night of the trip — see below.

Optional: sport fishing, ATV, zip-line, surfOptional: Carara National Park and pool dayCelebration NightOptional: private boat celebration
04
Manuel Antonio National Park · Quepos

Wildlife by Day, Bonfire by Night

A guided walk along the white-sand beaches of Manuel Antonio to spot sloths, monkeys and birds. In the afternoon, options on the water: a sunset catamaran with snorkeling and dolphins, a trip to Nauyaca Waterfalls, or a mangrove tour. As night falls, a bonfire on the beach: one last toast to the guest of honor, stories from the trip and a keepsake for everyone to take home. Then, farewell dinner.

Guided national park tourOptional: catamaran, waterfalls or mangrovesBeach bonfire and toast

Jacó → San José International Airport

Heading Home, with Stories for Years

One last easy morning together, then a private transfer back to SJO airport, with a stop for breakfast or lunch on the way. The team sees the group off, and everyone leaves with a lot more than a suitcase.

The only difference between the three tiers is where you sleep each night. Everything below is included, no matter which one your group picks.

  • All ground transportation and transfers, in our own vehicles (one or two, depending on the group)
  • Celebration Night

  • Water, energy drinks, a welcome beer and snacks throughout the trip

A deposit plus payment plan is offered at booking, as is standard for group travel in Costa Rica.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Who is this trip for?+
Groups of men celebrating something important — a 40th or 50th birthday, a promotion, a retirement, a class reunion or simply getting the old crew back together. Fathers and sons are welcome too. Most groups are 6 to 8 guests.
How is Celebration Night personalized for the guest of honor?+
A few days before the trip, Melvin talks with the guest of honor or the group organizer to learn the story and what this celebration means. He uses that to shape the Day 1 welcome toast, Celebration Night and a special moment just for him — so it's never a generic toast.
Can we choose our own activities?+
Yes. The route stays the same, but activities are chosen with your group before the trip — sport fishing, ATVs, zip-lining, surfing, horseback riding, a catamaran or just the pool. If your group wants something different, Melvin will find it and book it.
Can our group mix stay tiers?+
Yes. All three tiers share the same itinerary, host and Celebration Night — each guest picks the stay that works for him, and the whole group celebrates together every night.
I've heard Costa Rica is hot and humid — won't the activities be exhausting?+
We adjust the pace of each day to your group. No one has to do an activity they don't want to, and every adventure on Days 2 to 4 has a more relaxed option. Melvin and the drivers always have water and electrolytes on hand so everyone stays hydrated.
What's the safety and support plan?+
Every driver on this route is someone we already know and trust, and Melvin travels with the group and is available 24 hours a day. We know the nearest clinics and hospitals along the way, and we recommend every guest travel with insurance — ask Melvin for a recommendation when you book.
You're a new agency — how do we know you'll pull it off?+
Fair question, and we're less new than we look. We've spent almost three years organizing group trips around Costa Rica for our own friends and family, and this tour combines that experience with local partners who have been doing it even longer. We operate nine vetted vehicles in the San José area, with screened drivers who have spent the last two years driving these same roads.
Why isn't this trip advertised online or on social media?+
Because we keep it exclusive, on purpose. This tour is shared only through friends and trusted partners — which is exactly how it reached you. That personal trust is part of the experience.
Is there a payment plan, and what if plans change?+
Yes — Sneha Coastlines offers a deposit plus an installment plan at booking. Deposits are refundable up to a cutoff date before departure; reach out through the contacts below and the team will walk your group through the exact terms and dates.
